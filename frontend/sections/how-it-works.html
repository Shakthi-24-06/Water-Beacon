HOW IT WORKS

Get water updates in three simple steps.

Water Beacon helps you check your village RO water availability and purification quality before you leave home.

01

Search your village

Enter your village name in the search box or browse the stations directory to locate your local RO station.

02

Check water status & purity

See whether the plant has water dispensing, check the tank level %, and verify TDS purity metrics in real time.

03

Plan your collection

Visit the RO station with confidence when drinking water is dispensing, avoiding empty trips in hot weather.

Simple. Local. Reliable.

Status information is verified and updated through the Water Beacon system by village Panchayat operators.
